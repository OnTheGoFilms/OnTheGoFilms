One team.
Two countries.

On The Go Films, together with our partners in Japan, is now one production partner for both countries, opening up some of Asia's most remarkable locations from our offices in Mumbai, Jaipur and Tokyo.

We help you, your clients and your partners reach your production goals wherever you want to shoot. On cross-border shoots, one team on both sides makes your production plan and your budget far simpler.

Through our partnership with award-winning, Tokyo-based director and producer Anshul Chauhan, international productions get shared networks of crew, talent and local resources in both countries.

The On The Go Films India–Japan alliance brings two countries under one production team.

  • Creative

    Our teams know both countries and can help with creative briefs, scripts, casting and the cultural crossover between India, Japan and your home market.

  • Logistics

    We plan your production in one country or both, across several cities and regions: permits, crew, equipment, travel and locations.

  • Finance

    One point of contact and one budget. We act as a single hub for your production anywhere in India and Japan.
