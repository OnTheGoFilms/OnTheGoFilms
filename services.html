Services

Shoot in India & Japan. We'll handle the rest.

Shooting far from your home base means logistics are never far from your mind. Whatever the size of your production, filming in an unfamiliar country takes careful planning and real, on-the-ground knowledge of what's available locally, and what isn't.

Bringing a feature, series, documentary or commercial to India or Japan? From our offices in Mumbai, Jaipur and Tokyo, we give international producers one accountable local partner for budgets, permits, crew, locations and logistics, plus incentive filing for eligible projects.

What we handle

Mumbai · Jaipur · Tokyo

01

Production & Budgeting

We turn your script into a detailed budget in no time, then keep the shoot on track with daily cost reports and projections throughout the schedule.

02

Permissions & Permits

In India, foreign features and series get permission through India Cine Hub, plus local permits. Japan has no national filming permit, so each location is cleared separately. We prepare and file every permit for you.

03

Government Incentive Filing

We help you secure the best incentives available for your project, including the Government of India incentive of up to 40% of qualifying spend for foreign features, series and documentaries.

04

Locations

From Himalayan peaks and desert forts to neon-lit Tokyo streets, our researchers and our team suggest locations that suit your story, then scout and secure them for your shoot.

05

Crew Hiring

We put together experienced local crews, from DOPs and ADs to art, wardrobe, hair and make-up, and production assistants. Many speak English, and we add translators on set wherever needed.

06

Talent Casting

From lead roles to a street full of extras, we cast talent of every age and background for your story, working with trusted casting agencies in both countries.

07

Visas, Customs & Carnet

We help your cast and crew get the right visas to work on set, and handle customs clearance and ATA Carnet paperwork so your equipment arrives and leaves without delays.

08

Post-Production & VFX

We match you with edit, colour, sound and VFX partners across India and Japan, chosen for your project and budget: top technical teams and the best deals.
