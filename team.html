Producer

Sohrab Khodamorad Bozorgchami

Sohrab was born in Mumbai in 1987 into a Zoroastrian family. His values were shaped by his father’s distinguished aviation career, which spanned Khambatta Airlines (one of India’s oldest aviation names), British Airways and, later, helping to establish Kingfisher Airlines alongside Vijay Mallya. Growing up in that high-standard environment instilled in him deep-rooted values of service, kindness, professional hard work and a spirit of adventure.

Sohrab has nearly two decades of hands-on production experience in the Indian film and advertising industry. He began his journey as an assistant director and, from 2009 to 2017, worked alongside, assisted and line-produced for some of the country’s top commercial directors, including Sanjay Shetty, Nitesh Tiwari, Punit Malhotra, Suresh Triveni and Ashwiny Iyer Tiwari. He has since moved into long-format feature filmmaking, serving as part of the production leadership on major projects including Maidaan (directed by Amit Sharma).

For Sohrab, On The Go Films is far more than a brand name. As a producer, he champions a collaborative production ecosystem where world-class creative vision meets seamless logistics, so every project is delivered with speed, integrity and cinematic excellence.

Producer, Director & Cinematographer

Himman Dhamija

Himman Dhamija is a multifaceted filmmaker, director, producer and acclaimed cinematographer with over four decades of experience across Indian feature films, commercial campaigns and digital series. His best-known work includes Mangal Pandey, Bluffmaster, Roy and Kaalakaandi, to name a few from a never-ending list.

Having built his reputation on iconic, high-budget visual spectacles and nuanced drama, Himman brings a 360-degree creative vision to every project. His evolution from leading cinematographer to director and producer makes him a complete storyteller, combining sharp production instincts, dynamic visual direction and technical mastery.

Director & Producer

Anshul Chauhan

Anshul Chauhan was born in India. After attending military academy and graduating from MS University Vadodara with an Arts degree, he began working as an animator in 2006 at Paprikaas Studios (now Technicolor India), on Nickelodeon TV series such as Farm Kids and Back at the Barnyard. In 2008 he joined Crayon Pictures as an animator on the National Award-winning film Delhi Safari, and later became a team lead in Bangalore, overseeing production of the BBC TV series Everything’s Rosie.

In 2011 he relocated to Tokyo, where he worked at Polygon Pictures on projects including Disney XD’s Emmy Award-winning Tron: Uprising and the Transformers: Robots in Disguise TV series.

Anshul then pursued his passion for directing and producing. In 2017 he directed his first feature film, Bad Poetry Tokyo, which won the Grand Prix at the Brussels film festival and screened at many prestigious festivals, including Raindance. His second feature, Kontora (2019), received the Grand Prix at the Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival, along with multiple awards and critical acclaim at international festivals, including the prestigious Obayashi Award in 2020.

In 2021 Anshul directed and produced December, a courtroom drama that premiered at the Busan International Film Festival and was nominated for the Kim Ji-seok Award in 2022. The film screened at festivals around the world and had a successful theatrical run in Japan, with releases in Spain, Germany, South Korea and other countries. His latest Japanese feature, Tiger, is to be released soon.

Anshul is in early development on his debut Hollywood project, an MMA sports drama set in Las Vegas, in collaboration with CAA and Radical Media. He is also working on a new Japan–Taiwan project, recently selected for development by TCCF/TAICCA Taiwan and TTB Italy. Having recently finished shooting a documentary as director, producer and DoP, Anshul continues to push artistic boundaries.

Line Producer

Shivam Saxena

Shivam Saxena is an experienced Line Producer and Production Fixer with over 10 years of experience in the Indian film and advertising production industry.

He specialises in end-to-end production support, location scouting, permits and permissions, local crew coordination, equipment rentals, accommodation, transportation and on-ground logistics.

Unit Production Manager

Shashikant Singh

With over 25 years of hands-on experience in the film and advertising industry, Shashikant Singh is the operational backbone of our production services. Having managed line production and logistics on more than five feature films and a vast array of commercial campaigns, he brings an unmatched level of technical foresight, structural efficiency and industry expertise to every set.

Shashikant excels at seamless set management, ensuring shoot schedules run strictly on time, budgets are optimised and workflows remain frictionless. His deep-rooted industry network, sharp problem-solving and unwavering dedication make him an invaluable leader on set and a trusted partner for creators, directors and crew alike.
